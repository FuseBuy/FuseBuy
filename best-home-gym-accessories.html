What to Look For

  • Space: Choose equipment that fits your available workout area.
  • Durability: Look for materials designed to handle repeated use.
  • Adjustability: Adjustable equipment can be more versatile.
  • Storage: Compact equipment is easier to keep in a bedroom or apartment.
  • Safety: Use equipment according to the manufacturer's instructions and inspect it regularly.

FuseBuy Pick

For someone starting from scratch, we'd begin with resistance bands, an exercise mat, and a jump rope. They take up little space and can support a wide variety of workouts.
