What to Look For

  • Battery life: Consider how often you'll need to recharge.
  • Fit: Over-ear and on-ear designs feel different during long listening sessions.
  • Noise cancellation: ANC can be useful for travel and noisy environments.
  • Bluetooth: Check the supported Bluetooth version and device compatibility.
  • Microphone: If you take calls, check microphone quality and call features.
