What to Look For

  • Refresh rate: 120Hz or higher can make motion look noticeably smoother than 60Hz.
  • Resolution: 1080p is still a practical choice for budget gaming.
  • Response time: Look for a low advertised response time, while remembering that real-world performance can vary.
  • Adaptive sync: Compatibility with technologies such as FreeSync can help reduce screen tearing.
  • Size: 24 inches is a popular size for 1080p gaming.

FuseBuy Pick

For a budget PC gaming setup, we'd focus first on a 24-inch 1080p monitor with a refresh rate of at least 120Hz rather than paying extra for features you may not notice.
