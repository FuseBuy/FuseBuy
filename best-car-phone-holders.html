What to Look For

  • Mounting location: Decide between dashboard, windshield, CD slot, or air vent mounting.
  • Phone compatibility: Make sure the mount fits your phone and case.
  • Adjustability: A flexible arm can make navigation easier to see.
  • Stability: The mount should stay secure over bumps and turns.
  • Visibility: Never position a mount where it blocks your view of the road.

FuseBuy Pick

For most drivers, we'd start by comparing an adjustable dashboard mount with a simple vent mount. The best choice depends on your vehicle's interior and where you prefer your phone to sit.
