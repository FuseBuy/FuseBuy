What to Look For

  • Wattage: Higher wattage can support faster charging on compatible devices.
  • Ports: Multiple ports are useful when charging several devices.
  • Size: GaN chargers can provide high power in compact designs.
  • Compatibility: Check your device's maximum supported charging speed.
  • Cable: A charger does not always include the cable you need.

FuseBuy Pick

For a phone-only setup, a compact single-port USB-C charger is usually enough. If you're charging several devices, consider a multi-port charger with enough total wattage.
