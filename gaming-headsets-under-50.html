What Should You Look For?

  • Comfort: If you play for several hours, comfortable ear cushions and a reasonable weight matter.
  • Microphone: A clear microphone is important for Discord, multiplayer games and team communication.
  • Connection: Check whether your console or PC supports the headset's connection before buying.
  • Wired vs. wireless: Wired headsets usually offer more value at the $50 price point.
  • Price: Gaming headset prices change frequently, so check the current price before purchasing.

Our Pick

For most gamers shopping on a strict $50 budget, we'd start by comparing the HyperX Cloud Stinger 2 and Razer BlackShark V2 X. If you care more about PC features, the Logitech G432 is another strong option to consider.
