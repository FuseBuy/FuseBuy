What to Look For

  • Switches: Different mechanical switches provide different feels and sounds.
  • Layout: Compact keyboards save space, while full-size keyboards include more dedicated keys.
  • Wired connection: Wired keyboards usually offer strong value at this price.
  • Lighting: Backlighting can make gaming in darker rooms easier.
  • Build quality: Look for a keyboard that feels sturdy and doesn't flex excessively.
