What to Look For in a Gaming Mouse

  • Weight: Lighter mice can feel faster and easier to move.
  • Shape: Choose a shape that feels comfortable with your hand.
  • Sensor: A reliable sensor is more important than extremely high DPI numbers.
  • Buttons: Extra programmable buttons can be useful for games and everyday tasks.
  • Wired vs. wireless: Wired mice usually give you more features for your money at this price.
  • Grip: Consider whether you use palm, claw, or fingertip grip.
