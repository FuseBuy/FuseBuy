What to Look For

  • Power source: Many kits plug into a 12V outlet or USB port.
  • Controls: Decide whether you want a remote, buttons, or smartphone control.
  • Installation: Check how the strips attach before buying.
  • Brightness: Look for adjustable brightness so the lights aren't distracting.
  • Safety: Keep lighting positioned so it does not interfere with driving or visibility.

FuseBuy Pick

If you want the easiest customization, we'd compare app-controlled kits first. If you just want inexpensive ambient lighting, a basic LED strip kit can get the job done.
