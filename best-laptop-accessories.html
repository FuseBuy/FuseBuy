What to Look For

  • Compatibility: Check your laptop's ports and dimensions.
  • Portability: Choose accessories that fit how often you travel.
  • Build quality: Metal stands and sturdy hubs can provide better durability.
  • Ergonomics: A good stand and external mouse can improve desk comfort.
  • Organization: Cable management accessories can keep your setup cleaner.

FuseBuy Pick

If you're upgrading a basic laptop setup, we'd start with a laptop stand, wireless mouse, and USB-C hub. Those three accessories can make a noticeable difference without requiring a major investment.
